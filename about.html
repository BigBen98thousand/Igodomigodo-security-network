About us

Indigenous by design. Institutionally aligned by principle.

Igodomigodo Security Network Limited is a registered Edo-first security platform combining local legitimacy, infrastructure protection, riverine reach and command-room accountability.

Company profile

Security built on trust, not just equipment.

Security in Edo's energy and cultural corridors requires a hybrid model: indigenous intelligence for ground truth, professional standards for discipline, and digital systems for auditable response.

Igodomigodo is positioned as an Edo-first security platform — not a generic contractor. Communities are more likely to share intelligence with people they know, trust and can hold accountable, and that legitimacy reduces friction and improves the flow of information long before an incident escalates.

Strategic thesis

A credible Edo security partner helps preserve national production gains by preventing localised sabotage, protecting energy corridors, and feeding verified field intelligence into state and federal response channels.

Operating mandate

A scope broader than guarding or patrol-only contracts.

Critical infrastructure

Oil pipelines, gas pipelines, flow stations, gas plants, refineries and storage facilities.

Territorial integrity

Boundary corridors, riverine access routes and areas exposed to sabotage risk.

Cultural heritage

Respect for Edo cultural institutions and community leadership structures.

Community stability

Local intelligence networks, early warning and non-intimidating engagement.

Institutional alignment

ISN contributes trusted local intelligence, early warning, verification, documentation and community interface.

 

It does not replace statutory agencies, approved infrastructure operators or existing security contractors. Enforcement remains with authorised institutions.
