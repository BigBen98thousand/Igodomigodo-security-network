The mandate

People, infrastructure, territory and heritage.

Igodomigodo is positioned as an Edo-first security platform, not a generic contractor. The profile combines local legitimacy, infrastructure protection, riverine reach and command-room accountability.

Communities are more likely to share intelligence with people they know, trust and can hold accountable — that legitimacy is the differentiator equipment alone can't provide.

Critical infrastructure

Oil pipelines, gas pipelines, flow stations, gas plants, refineries and storage facilities.

Territorial integrity

Boundary corridors, riverine access routes and areas exposed to sabotage risk.

Cultural heritage

Respect for Edo cultural institutions and community leadership structures.

Community stability

Local intelligence networks, early warning and non-intimidating engagement.

Operating model

A 3-layer model converts local knowledge into accountable field action.

01

Community intelligence

Trusted local observers, traditional structures and early-warning channels identify anomalies before escalation.

02

Technology verification

Drones, sensors, GPS patrol trails and digital incident reports validate and timestamp field intelligence.

03

Coordinated response

Command-desk triage, evidence capture and agency liaison support authorised response and a shared operating picture.

Service portfolio

Six service families, one complete partner.

Prevention, protection and long-term capacity — structured beyond guard deployment.

Intelligence & Risk

Threat detection, community intelligence, pipeline corridor monitoring and territorial analysis.

Consulting & Advisory

Pipeline protection strategy, crisis planning, cultural risk assessment and engagement frameworks.

Technology Integration

Drone surveillance, motion sensors, GPS patrol systems, CCTV and incident platforms.

Asset Protection

Pipelines, gas plants, flow stations, refineries, storage, routes and boundary corridors.

Devices & Equipment

Drones, sensors, patrol boats, GPS trackers, night vision, communications and PPE.

Training & Capacity

Pipeline surveillance, riverine navigation, intelligence gathering and emergency response.
